## Description

---

Need a little celebration in your design? Confetti Genie sprinkles confetti over any frame in one click: rectangles, ellipses, polygons and stars, in your brand colors or a bright random palette.

Works in Figma Design, FigJam, Figma Slides and Figma Buzz.


What's new

One tidy layer: all the confetti goes into a single "Confetti" layer, so it's easy to move, hide or delete
Stretches with your frame: resize the frame and the confetti spreads to fit, even in auto layout frames
Regenerate: Confetti layers get a "Regenerate confetti" button in the right panel for a fresh layout with the same settings
Quick actions: type "Confetti Genie" in Figma's quick actions, pick a density, done. No window needed
Remembers your settings: density, size, shapes and your colors are there next time
Light and dark: follows your Figma theme, or pick one yourself
Keyboard friendly: every control works with the keyboard and screen readers


Features

Four confetti shapes: rectangles, ellipses, polygons (3 to 6 sides) and stars (4 to 6 points), in any mix
Density from minimal to blast, and Small, Medium or Large pieces
Your own colors, or a random palette of 14 bright colors. Add a color twice to make it more common
Varying opacity and rotation for natural-looking confetti
Select several frames, groups or sections to fill them all at once
Nothing selected? A new frame is created in the middle of your view
Generate again with a Confetti layer selected to add more


How to use

1. Select one or more frames, groups or sections (or nothing, to create a new frame)
2. Choose the shapes, density and size
3. Add your colors and click "Generate with selected colors", or click "Generate with random colors"

Faster: open quick actions (Ctrl/⌘ + /), type "Confetti Genie", press Tab, and pick a density. Size and colors are optional.


Good to know

Confetti goes inside layers that can hold other layers: frames, groups, sections and components. Instances and component sets are skipped, since Figma doesn't allow adding layers to them.
